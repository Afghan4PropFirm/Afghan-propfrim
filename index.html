COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions, answered clearly

A preview of the information traders should have before any future launch.

Are the account balances real?

No. All balances in this prototype are simulated and are not funds held for a trader.

Can I buy a challenge today?

No. Yes. Select a plan, transfer the exact amount of USDT on BNB Smart Chain (BEP20), and send the transaction hash to the manager. Your MT5 details are sent by email after manual review.

Does this website create an MT5 account?

Not yet. Automatic MT5 account creation requires an authorized broker or infrastructure provider and has not been connected.

Are future rewards guaranteed?

No. Any future reward program would require clear published terms, eligibility checks and sufficient verified funds.
